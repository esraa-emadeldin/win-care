# Code of Conduct

win-care follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in win-care to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their
age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new — many of us were students once.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may ban people from the project, temporarily or permanently.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or a private security advisory on this repository. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
