# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Smart Scan: junk files, apps, startup items and health in one scan.
- Review before deleting; everything goes to the Recycle Bin, never deleted permanently by accident.
- Applications: uninstall with the app's own uninstaller, then review its leftovers.
- Performance: switch per-user startup apps on and off (Task Manager compatible), restart
  Explorer, empty the Recycle Bin, read-only health checks.
- Space Map: treemap of a drive; send a folder to Review.
- Tray popup with live CPU, RAM, disk, battery and network.
- Start-up splash; light and dark themes; opt-in daily update check.
